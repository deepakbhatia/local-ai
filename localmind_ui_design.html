The parties acknowledge that the obligations set forth herein constitute reasonable protections of legitimate business interests. For a period of twenty-four (24) months following the termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in any business activity substantially similar to that of the Disclosing Party within a fifty (50) mile radius of the Disclosing Party's principal place of business.
